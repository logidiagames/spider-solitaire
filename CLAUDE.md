## Spider rules (all in `FreeCellCore`, between `freecell-core:start` and `freecell-core:end`)

- Board: `{tableau, stock, foundations, done, suits}`. Ten columns of `{s, r, up, id}` (`up:false` = face down; `id` 0–103 tells the two copies of a card apart, so board elements and saved games stay stable), the stock as `{s, r, id}` with the next row last (the last card goes to the first column), `foundations` eight slots (13 once a run fills it, in the order the runs are made), `done` the suit of each completed run, `suits` 1, 2 or 4.
- Tiers keep FreeCell's keys so the economy, rivals, missions, Java `GameConfig` and the 18 languages carry over: `easy` = 1 suit (8 × spades), `medium` = 2 suits (4 × spades and hearts), `hard` = 4 suits. Names on screen: "1 Suit", "2 Suits", "4 Suits" (`tier_*`). Default for a new player: `easy` (`difficulty_config.default_tier`); the Daily is 2 suits on Sunday and Saturday, 1 suit on weekdays.
- `dealGame(seed, suits)`: Microsoft's LCG shuffles the 104 cards; 54 are dealt row by row into ten columns (6 in the first four, 5 in the others; last card face up), 50 make the stock. `newBoard(seed, suits)`. 
- `canMove` is the one move rule (game, solver, hint): a face-up run of one suit in order moves onto any card one rank higher (any suit) or into an empty column; dealing (`DEAL`: stock → one face-up card on every column) needs every column to hold a card. `applyMove` turns the uncovered face-down card face up and sends a complete King-to-Ace run of one suit to the next foundation by itself (`collectRuns`), also after a deal.
- Scoring (`SCORING`, `moveScore`): 500 at the start, −1 per move (a deal and an Undo included), +100 per complete run; never below 0.
- Solver: the FreeCell best-first search on Spider moves (`listMoves` leaves out a run that already sits on the next card of its suit; `analyse` retries with every move before saying "unsolvable"). It knows the face-down cards and the stock. `tools/spider_solver_check.mjs` replays it on fresh deals: on the PC 1 suit is solved in about 0.2 s, 2 suits about two thirds of deals within 150,000 positions, 4 suits almost never (the hint then falls back to its best guess, as FreeCell does when the budget runs out).
- Deal catalog (`assets/deal_catalog.json`, version 3, `tools/rate_deal_ease.mjs`; owner request 2026-11-17: the first games were too hard): deal numbers for `easy` and `medium`, **sorted easiest first for a person**: a simple player (same-suit moves first, then moves that turn a card, deals when nothing useful is left) plays each deal 24 times, and the share it wins is the deal's ease. 400 / 200 deals spread over the whole range of winnable deals (6,116 of 6,400 fresh 1 Suit deals were won at least once), so band 0 is the easiest fifth. The solver's effort (`tools/rank_deal_catalog.mjs`, version 2) did not match how hard a deal feels; `hard` has no list (any number). The Daily draws from the easier three fifths of its tier's list.
- Adaptive difficulty (owner request 2026-11-14: deals start easy and get harder with the player's win rate): each list is cut into five bands (`ADAPT_BANDS`); the saved `adaptLevel` per tier starts at 0 (easiest band), the five warm-up games do not count (owner request 2026-11-17); after them a Classic win adds half a band, a Classic game left unwon after a move (a new deal started, not a Restart) takes a band off, so the deals settle where the player wins about two games in three (`adaptAfter`, `adaptBand`, `bandSlice`, `pickDeal` with `band`). Classic games draw from their band (unplayed first, never one of the last 50).
- Warm-up (owner requests 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-17): the first five Classic games are fixed, all easy 1 Suit deals (`difficulty_config.first_games.seeds` 4191935, 4931420, 3437399, 8831351, 6156738; the simple player wins them 75–96 % of the time). Same seeds in the page DEFAULTS, `difficulty_config_defaults.json`, Java `GameConfig` and the published Firebase parameter.
- No Game by Number (owner request 2026-11-14): removed from the New Game menu and Settings (its collection), with its mission and achievements; only the Daily is shared. The mode code stays for old saves.
- Spider targets (owner request 2026-11-15; the solver wins 1 Suit in about 110 moves and 2 Suits in about 160, deals included): 3 stars up to 180 moves and 720 s; missions `moves_180`, `fast_15` (900 s), `fast_10` (600 s), `cards_52` and `cards_104` (runs leave 13 cards at a time); `winnable_2` removed (no Winnable mode; the type stays for old saves); Daily Rivals `median_seconds` 600 with tier multipliers 0.8 / 1 / 1.6. Same values in the page DEFAULTS, the `*_defaults.json` files and Java `GameConfig` fallbacks.
- No Autocomplete and no card-by-card foundation moves (runs leave by themselves): `canAutocomplete` is always false and the "Auto-move to foundation" setting is gone. "Stock position" replaces "Free cell position" (`options.stockRight`).

## Table

- Look (owner request 2026-11-17, from a photo of the classic game): cards keep a card's shape: `measureColumnRoom` sets `--card-w` (the column width) and `--card-h` = width × 1.3 / 1.38 / 1.46 (Normal / Large / Extra large; the owner found 1.42 still too long), at most 58 / 66 / 74 px; strips scale with it (`cardMetrics`). The default Classic face is a light ivory (`#f8f3e7`→`#efe6d2`), the centre suit is bigger (94cqw), ranks in Nunito ExtraBold. Face cards (owner requests 2026-11-17): the small suit sits in the top right corner, the rank is 90 % of the size, and the picture is at the bottom right, as large as the room beside the rank (under the suit) or under the ink of the letter allows (`--court-scale` for J and K, `--court-scale-q` for the Q and its tail, class `queen`; measured on a drawn card, 1 px clear). The staircase Kings fill their slot (no border) so they get the same picture size.
- Top row (same request): the stock is a fan of card backs, one per deal left (`.stock-back`, 30 % of a card apart), on the right; the complete runs pile up on the left as a staircase of Kings (foundation slots absolutely placed, `--i`, `.filled`). "Stock position" (default right) mirrors them. Ten columns; face-down cards show a thin strip, face-up ones a wider one, squeezed when a column would run past its room (`columnTops`).
- Highlight movable cards is on by default (owner request 2026-11-17); only a choice made in Settings is kept (`highlightChosen`). It darkens only covered face-up cards that cannot move: the front card of a column is never darkened. The automatic hint (after 15 s without a move) makes the run and its destination blink once, no border (`markHintMove`, `.hint`, `.hint-target`, `spider-blink`), and its label is large in the middle of the table (`showMessage(text,{hint:true})`, `.hint-label`).
- A complete run flies to its foundation Ace first (`flyCompletedRuns`) with the suit-complete sound, haptic and ring; a deal slides the ten cards from the stock (`animateDealRow`). A tap moves a run onto the next card of its own suit first, then any next card, then an empty column (`autoMove`). The hint names the run's top card or pulses the stock ("Deal a new row from the stock").
- Saved game (`remember` / `restoreSavedGame`): tableau with face-down flags and ids, stock, foundations, done, suits, score; `validBoard` checks every one of the 104 ids appears once. Saves from FreeCell are not read (another app).
- Tutorial (6 steps) and Rules are redrawn for Spider (`TUTORIAL_COLUMNS`, `miniBoardMarkup`, `rulesView`).

## Languages

English is built in. Start language (owner decision 2026-11-15): the device language when we have it (pt-PT phones get Portuguese, a German phone German…), else English; a language chosen in Settings or Android's per-app language wins (`pickLanguage`). All 18 languages have every new or changed Spider text (rules, tutorial, tier names, stock texts, the game name in share and rating texts): `tools/spider_texts.json`, applied with `node tools/apply_spider_texts.mjs`. The 15 non-Portuguese ones (es, fr, it, de, nl, pl, tr, ru, uk, ja, ko, zh-Hans, id, sv, cs) are first drafts that need a native review. They use the local game name where one is usual (Solitario Spider, Spider Solitär, Pasjans Pająk, Örümcek Solitaire, пасьянс «Паук», пасьянс «Павук», スパイダーソリティア, 스파이더 솔리테어, 蜘蛛纸牌, Pavouk; Spider Solitaire in fr, nl, id, sv); the launcher name (`app_name`) follows the device language the same way: `res/values-<lang>/strings.xml` for pt (both Portuguese), es, it, de, pl, tr, ru, uk, ja, ko, `b+zh+Hans`, cs; "Spider Solitaire" elsewhere. Fix a text in `tools/spider_texts.json` and rerun the script, never in the packs directly.
