FreeCell v1.2 — store items for approval

Status: backgrounds, card faces, card backs and victory effects were approved on 23 October 2026 and are in the game. The Winter Festival rewards at the end of this page were approved the same day and are in the game as event items.

Proposed items for the Style store, drawn exactly as the game would draw them (CSS only, no images). Each one sits on a small board with both suit colours, empty slots and the "no more moves" message, so you can judge readability. Prices are suggestions inside the brief's ranges; the final prices go in store_config.

Card faces show their measured contrast: rank and suit against the card must be at least 4.5:1 (WCAG). Red and black suits differ by shape (♥♦ against ♠♣) and by lightness, not only by hue: each face's red is the lightest red that still keeps 4.5:1, and the lightness gap is shown (0.10 or more passes). No glows or blurred shadows on any text.

Winter Festival event (approved)

Exclusive event rewards: never sold, shown in the Style store with an Event tag, and unlocked by winning on 10 different days between 1 December and 6 January (values in events_config; the event ships switched off). The snowflakes are inline SVG, so they stay crisp at any size.
