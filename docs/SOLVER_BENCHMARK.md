# Hint solver benchmark

Generated by `node tools/solver_benchmark.js 100` on 2026-10-30 (Node v24.22.1, desktop PC).
Each deal is analysed from its start with the default `hint_config` budget (300 ms,
400,000 positions), as the Hint button does. Every line found was replayed through the
game's move rule (`FreeCellCore.canMove`) to a win.

| Deals | Count | Line found | Median | 90% | Max | Line found with 75 ms ("slow phone") |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | 5 | 100% | 7.4 ms | 10 ms | 10 ms | 100% |
| Easy | 100 | 100% | 4.6 ms | 15 ms | 188 ms | 99% |
| Medium | 100 | 98% | 6.7 ms | 32 ms | 304 ms | 96% |
| Hard | 100 | 96% | 24 ms | 175 ms | 303 ms | 79% |

Notes:

- A deal start is the hardest moment: later in a game the search space is much smaller, and the
  background analysis after each move usually has the answer ready before the player taps Hint.
- When no line is found within the budget the hint falls back to the best guess and nothing is
  claimed; only an exhaustive search reports a lost position.
- Real phone times come from the debug timing log (DEV panel, row "Solver", and `[solver]` lines
  in logcat).
